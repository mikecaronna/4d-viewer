4D capture viewer

Volumetric video from a camera array, played back in the browser. Orbit with the mouse, scrub the timeline.

How to move around

  • Left-drag to orbit.
  • Scroll to move in and out. Note it moves in when you scroll up.
  • The bar along the bottom plays and scrubs time.
  • Stay near the middle. Every camera pointed inward, so nothing outside the ring they formed was ever photographed, and the view breaks down if you travel out past them.

What this is

Forty-one small cameras on stands, arranged around the subject, all recording at once. The footage is reconstructed into a cloud of coloured ellipsoids that each carry a position, a moment in time and a velocity, so the scene moves rather than being a sequence of separate frames.

Honest limitations

  • Colour does not change with viewing angle. The compressed format drops the higher-order terms that would carry that.
  • The file downloads whole before anything appears. There is no progressive loading.
  • The camera stands have been removed from the guitar scene, so the performer can be seen from the front without the rig in the way. A few stray ellipsoids remain further out.
